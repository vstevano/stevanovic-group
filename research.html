Research

Four threads run through the group's work — most tied together by a first-principles view of why materials form the structures they do, why do they have properteis they do, and what happens when they don't sit at equilibrium.

Stability and metastability

First-principles materials thermodynamics, thermochemistry and synthesizability. Methods for accurate compound enthalpies of formation, their Gibbs free energy, sampling crystalline polymorphs with random structure sampling, and predicting the kinetics of polymorphic transformations via crystal structure mapping. Key results: answering why there are no metastable polymorphs among binary rocksalts, new mechanism for the martensitic transformation in steel, atom-to-atom mapping algorithm between 3D periodic structures shown to be very useful in predicting bulk mechanisms of polymorphic transformations, synthsizability of polymorphs and the resolution of the contraversy between the relatively small number of observed metastable polymorphs and a large number of available local minima, the FERE method for computing accurate compound enthsalpies of formation.

Representative: J. Am. Chem. Soc. 146, 23004 (2024); Applied Physics Reviews 8, 031310 (2021); Phys. Rev. Lett. 125, 125502 (2020); J. Chem. Phys. 152, 074106 (2020); Phys. Rev. Lett. 116, 075503 (2016); Nat. Commun. 9, 4168 (2018); Phys. Rev. B 85, 115104 (2012)

Amorphous materials

Representation of an amorphous solid as a composite (ensemble) of small-cell local domains rather than a single (or several) large supercells. Domains obtained via the random structure sampling and properties computed as ensemble averages. The approach enables the use of high-accuracy electronic structure methods (GW and GW+BSE) and the accurate predictions of the electronic structure, disorder-limited transport, optical properties, etc.

Representative: Nat. Phys. 22, 88 (2026); J. Appl. Phys. 137, 095101 (2025); Adv. Mater. 37, 2501074 (2025)

Wide gap semiconductors

Wide- and ultrawide-bandgap oxide and nitride semiconductors for high-power electronics. Identification of novel candidate materials, dopability, equilibria with contacts, modeling interface atomic and electronic structure.

Representative: Science Advances 9, eade3761 (2023); PRX Energy 1, 033006 (2022); Phys. Rev. Applied 16, 064064 (2021); Energy Environ. Sci. 12, 3338 (2019)

Other stuff

A few threads that don't fold neatly into the areas above:

Defect physics & doping. Point-defect thermodynamics and dopability in oxide and nitride semiconductors, built on defect-calculation frameworks developed and automated in the group — including the fitted elemental-phase reference energies (FERE) approach for accurate formation enthalpies.

Thermoelectrics. Computational identification and design of thermoelectric materials, including the TE Design Lab virtual laboratory.

Computational methods & software. Open tools for pylada, a Python environment for high-throughput first-principles calculations. Tools developed in Stevanović group include modules for point-defect automation, polymorphic transformation kinetics, structural analysis, surface cutting, etc.

Representative: Phys. Rev. B 85, 115104 (2012); Nature Reviews Materials 2, 17053 (2017); npj Comput. Mater. 8, 172 (2022); Comp. Mat. Sci. 130, 1 (2017)
